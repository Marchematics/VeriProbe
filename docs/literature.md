
# Literature map (verified through 2026-10-04)

1. Shanshan Zhong, Kate Shen, Chenyan Xiong. **AgentWebBench: Benchmarking Multi-Agent Coordination in Agentic Web.** ICML 2026. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/zhong26n.html
   - Supplies the decentralized content-agent setting, but assumes cooperative access.
2. Olivier Martinez. **Optimizing Visibility in Generative Engines: A Critical Survey of Generative Engine Optimization (2023-2026).** arXiv:2607.14035.
   - Documents rapidly growing incentives to optimize generative-engine visibility.
3. Haozhang Li et al. **When Optimization Becomes Manipulation: Defending Generative Search against Malicious Generative Engine Optimization.** arXiv:2609.02964.
   - Strong detector/reranker-style defense family; a required baseline.
4. Strategic information retrieval / mechanism-design literature on publishers adapting to ranking mechanisms.
   - Establishes that content providers are strategic actors, not passive documents.

## Novelty boundary
The method must not be marketed as another GEO detector. Its novelty is the **challenge-before-routing mechanism**: selection is conditioned on randomized, post-commit verified evidence rather than strategic declarations or static detector features.
